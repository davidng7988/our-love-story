# Our Love Story 💕 - Days & Memories

A warm, cute, mobile-first website celebrating our journey together from our very first day until forever.

🔗 **Live GitHub Repository**: [https://github.com/davidng7988/our-love-story](https://github.com/davidng7988/our-love-story)  
🌐 **GitHub Pages (After enabling)**: [https://davidng7988.github.io/our-love-story/](https://davidng7988.github.io/our-love-story/)

---

## ✨ Features

- **💑 Real-Time Live Love Counter**:
  - Baseline timestamp: **April 27, 2024 at 11:34 PM**.
  - Dynamically calculates the exact difference from when you got together until now.
  - Live ticking **Years, Months, Days, Hours, Minutes, and Seconds** with smooth animations.
  - Total days counter (e.g. over 560+ days together!).
- **📱 Mobile-Native First Experience**:
  - Built specifically to look and feel like a modern, polished mobile app on your phone.
  - Touch-friendly Polaroid photo cards, rounded pill badges, safe area support, and responsive fluid layout.
- **🗓️ Daily Markdown (Interactive Calendar & Activity Logger)**:
  - Interactive monthly calendar with previous/next month navigation and "Today" quick-jump.
  - Visual activity badges on days (e.g. 🏸 Sport, 🍽️ Dates, ✈️ Trips, 📸 Photos).
  - Selected date viewer with relative date tag ("Today", "Yesterday", "3 days ago").
  - Log daily activities:
    - **Sport & Fitness**: Badminton 🏸, Gym 🏋️, Jogging 🏃, Swimming 🏊, Cycling 🚴, Tennis 🎾, etc.
    - **Date & Food 🍽️**, **Movie & Chill 🎬**, **Trip & Outing ✈️**, **Sweet Moment 💖**, **Daily Journal 📝**.
  - Built-in **Markdown notes editor** supporting `**bold**`, `*italic*`, `- bullet lists`, and `> quotes`.
  - **Optional photo upload**: Attach sweet daily photos with client-side canvas compression for rapid sync.
  - Fullscreen photo lightbox so both partners can view the photo in high resolution!
  - Filter by Sport, Date, or Photos only, plus toggle between Calendar and Timeline List views.
  - Multi-device real-time sync with Google Cloud Firestore backend.
- **📸 Cherished Memories Timeline**:
  - Pre-populated with your special moments:
    1. **Our Very First Date 🍽️**: D'Fortune Western Restaurant in Manjalara
    2. **Second Date ⛰️**: Chilly breezes & warm hugs at Genting Highlands
    3. **First Trip Together 🍜🏖️**: Road trip and food hunting in Penang
    4. **Second Trip 💦🌴**: Lost World of Tambun, Ipoh
    5. **Third Trip (Recent) 🎢🇸🇬**: Singapore & Universal Studios Singapore
  - Polaroid style frame with dedicated space for photos.
  - Filter by All, Dates, or Trips.
- **⚙️ Admin & Photo Upload Console**:
  - Accessible via the `⚙️` button in the header or bottom of page.
  - Direct image uploading from your phone/computer (camera or photo album).
  - High-res image optimization and persistent storage in browser `IndexedDB`.
  - Add new memories anytime as your journey continues!
  - Edit names, change anniversary baseline date/time, and update love quotes.
  - Export and download memories as JSON or backup anytime.
- **🌸 Romantic Aesthetic & Interactions**:
  - Gentle floating pastel heart particles in the background.
  - Tap anywhere on the screen or click `💕` for a burst of mini hearts!
  - Built-in soothing lullaby/melody player (`🎵` button) using the Web Audio API without needing external audio files.

## 🚀 How to Run Locally

Simply open `index.html` in any web browser! No installation or local server required.

Or if you prefer using a local HTTP server:
```bash
npx serve .
```

## 🌐 How to Enable GitHub Pages (To share with your girlfriend)

1. Go to your repository on GitHub: [https://github.com/davidng7988/our-love-story](https://github.com/davidng7988/our-love-story)
2. Click on **Settings** (tab at top).
3. On the left sidebar, click **Pages**.
4. Under **Build and deployment** > **Branch**, select `main` (or `master`) and folder `/ (root)`.
5. Click **Save**.
6. Within 1-2 minutes, your website will be live at:
   `https://davidng7988.github.io/our-love-story/`
7. Send the link to her or open it on her phone and add to Home Screen!
